## Notable movers & why

### 🔻 Decliners

- **HUMA (Humacyte) — 1W −36%, 1M −44%, volume +98%.** Priced a ~$50M underwritten
  public offering (47.6M shares at $1.05), heavy dilution from a sub-$1 base. The
  drop came *despite* positive clinical news — its hemodialysis AV-access study met
  its primary superiority endpoint, with a supplemental BLA planned for H2 2026.
  Market focused on the raise. ([StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/HUMA/))
- **SATL (Satellogic) — 1W −26%, 1M −57%.** Mnuchin-linked entities sold ~$97.7M of
  stock; CFO Rick Dunn announced departure; SpaceX's IPO debut pressured space peers.
  An $18M defense Earth-observation contract and a new board member weren't enough to
  offset cash-burn concerns. ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/why-satellogic-satl-down-17-081619716.html))
- **AXTI (AXT Inc) — 1W −25%, 1M −48%.** Compound-semiconductor (InP) substrate maker
  for AI/data-center. Record >$100M backlog, but growth hinges on unpredictable
  Chinese export-permit timing (capacity sits in its Tongmei subsidiary) — management
  called permits the "most significant single factor," "not in our control."
  ([MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NASDAQ/AXTI/news/))
- **SIDU (Sidus Space) — 1W −23%, 1M −60%.** Closed a $100M best-efforts registered
  direct offering (~19.7M Class A shares + warrants at $5.08) — large float increase
  read as dilution. ([Timothy Sykes](https://www.timothysykes.com/news/sidus-space-inc-sidu-news-2026_06_12/))
- Also weak: **FLNC −17% 1W**, **AAOI −17%**, **AMPX −16%**, **LODE −15%**,
  **SLOIF −12%**, **XIACY −12%** — broad pullback in the speculative cohort.

### 🔺 Gainers

- **SNDK (SanDisk) — 1W +19%, 1M +47%, volume +38%.** Surged after rival **Micron**
  crushed fiscal-Q3 estimates ($25.11 EPS, sales ~$41.5B, +4x YoY), reinforcing the
  AI-memory demand thesis. Now a high-volatility momentum/WSB vehicle — expect sharp
  reversals. ([TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261990267-sandisk-sndk-stock-analysis-micron-ai-memory-demand-june-2026-tradingkey))
- **MOH (Molina Healthcare) — 1W +11%, 1M +23%, volume +85%.** BofA upgraded to Buy
  (PT $250 from $152); Illinois Medicaid managed-care contract win and improving Q1
  margins. ([GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8932654/molina-healthcare-inc-moh-stock-up-73-and-still-undervalued-gf-score-80100))
- Also firm: **VENU +14% 1W**, **PENG +12%**, **BE +9%** (note BE was −11% over 3D —
  choppy), **HFCL.NS +8%**, **COHR +8%**.
